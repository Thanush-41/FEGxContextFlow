# Impact case

ContextFlow tests whether a user-chosen task can remain useful across research, voice, app, widget, and live-activity surfaces without measuring success solely by betting volume.

The proposed evaluation is a 1,000-person pilot split equally between control and treatment, with 30 days of exposure and later retention follow-up. Primary measures are useful app opens, widget adoption, surface engagement, retained notification consent, task completion, off-app completion, opt-outs, failures, and harmful-play indicators.

The editable scenario model is [`submission/review/ContextFlow_Impact_Model.xlsx`](../submission/review/ContextFlow_Impact_Model.xlsx). It models 10%, 20%, and 30% adoption among 500 treatment participants. The illustrative 20% case has 100 adopters, 400 voice tasks per month, about EUR 258 monthly operating cost, and EUR 1.58 required monthly contribution per adopter after assumed support savings. Illustrative upfront integration and assurance effort is EUR 9,120. These are assumptions for pilot planning, not measured ROI; low adoption does not pay back, and usage, negotiated pricing, identity services, and production integration still require validation.
